This plugin helps you to calculate profit for your WooCommerce products by tracking buying prices and generating detailed reports.

== Description ==

The **Samrat Profit Calculator for WooCommerce** is a powerful yet simple tool for store owners to track their actual net profit. By allowing you to set a cost basis (Buying Price) for every product, the plugin automatically calculates your margins for every order.

### Key Features:
* **Buying Price Field:** Add a cost price to any WooCommerce product (simple or variation).
* **Profit Reports:** View a comprehensive table of orders with selling price, buying price, and net profit.
* **Date Filtering:** Filter your profit reports by year, month, or specific date ranges.
* **PDF Export:** Generate professional PDF profit reports with a single click.
* **Historical Accuracy:** Captures the buying price at the moment of purchase, so your reports remain accurate even if you change product prices later.

== Installation ==

1. Upload the plugin files to the `/wp-content/plugins/samrat-profit-calculator-for-woocommerce/` directory.
2. Activate the plugin through the 'Plugins' menu in WordPress.
3. Edit any product and go to the **General** tab to enter the **Buying Price**.
4. Navigate to **WooCommerce > Profit Calculation** to view your reports.
